About Artificial Influence

Artificial Influence and Human Quotient are two views of the same production relationship. One starts with AI involvement; the other starts with human involvement. Neither endpoint is presented as better.

Artificial Influence describes how AI shaped a piece of work. It is not a quality score or a judgment.
