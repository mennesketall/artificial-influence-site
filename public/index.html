A measure of how much
AI shaped the work.

Artificial Influence is a 0–10 scale that describes the role AI played in creating a piece of content. It is neither an AI detector nor a quality score. It describes a process, not a value judgment.

The Scale

0 means all human; 10 means all AI. The levels between describe different relationships between human creation and AI involvement. See how this relates to Human Quotient →

  • AI0All human
    The work was created directly by a person, without AI shaping the output.    view →
  • AI1Human-led
    A person created the work and used AI only around the edges, without shaping the final substance.    view →
  • AI2Human-directed
    A person led the work; AI helped with limited supporting tasks.    view →
  • AI3Human-shaped
    A person substantially shaped AI-assisted material into the final work.    view →
  • AI4Mixed creation
    Human-created and AI-created parts were combined and reviewed as one work.    view →
  • AI5AI-assisted
    AI contributed meaningfully while a person directed and revised the result.    view →
  • AI6AI-guided
    AI produced a starting point or substantial material that a person directed and developed.    view →
  • AI7AI-shaped
    AI produced much of the material; a person made significant changes and decisions.    view →
  • AI8AI-produced
    AI produced the work with human direction limited to prompts, selection, or light revision.    view →
  • AI9Mostly AI
    AI produced nearly all of the work; human involvement was limited.    view →
  • AI10All AI
    The work was produced entirely by AI, with no meaningful human shaping of the output.    view →

Artificial Influence describes how AI shaped a piece of work. It is not a quality score or a judgment.
